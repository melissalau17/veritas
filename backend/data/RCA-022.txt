ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS: RCA-022

Asset: P-205
Equipment family: CP-100
Incident Date: 2025-02-14

Problem:
High vibration was observed during routine operation on transfer pump.

Root Cause:
Bearing degradation due to lubricant contamination.

Corrective Action:
Replaced drive-end bearing and renewed grease lubrication per MNT-008.

Result:
Vibration levels normalized to baseline.

Note:
Applies to CP-100 light-duty pump series.
